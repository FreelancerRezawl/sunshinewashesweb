Frequently Asked Questions

Here are the answers to the most common questions regarding memberships and facilities.

How often can I wash my car with a membership?
As an Unlimited Wash Club member, you can wash your vehicle once per day, every day the wash is open, at any of our Sunshine Washes locations.
Can I manage my account online?
Yes! You can manage your account 24/7 in our customer portal. You can securely update your payment method, view billing records, swap vehicles, or pause your membership at any time.
Is the tunnel wash safe for electric and hybrid vehicles?
Absolutely. Our tunnel and sensors are fully compatible with electric, hybrid, and conventional internal combustion vehicles. Please ensure your vehicle is placed in neutral and wipers are switched off.
