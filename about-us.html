Our Story

Family-owned and proudly serving the Tampa Bay area with the highest quality express tunnel washing since 2011.

Shining Bright in Tampa Bay

Founded with a vision to deliver a premium, fast, and eco-friendly car wash experience, Sunshine Washes has grown to become Tampa Bay's favorite neighborhood express wash. Over the past decade, we have remained committed to two primary principles: unmatched customer care and state-of-the-art tunnel technology.

Our facilities utilize water reclamation technology that cleans and filters over 80% of the water we use, ensuring we conserve resources while washing away harsh road salts, bugs, and pollen from your vehicle's finish.

We believe that a clean vehicle is a happier drive, and we strive to provide that "reset" moment for every customer who passes through our gates.
